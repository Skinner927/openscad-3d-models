I need two sets of measurements from you before I can finish the design. A caliper or ruler is enough.

1. Male plug magnetic head: how wide and tall it is, and how far its face sticks out in +Y (toward the port) past
   the 6mm-thick clamp block. This decides whether the clamp and flexure hit the port plate when the plug is fully in.
2. Female bulkhead: the flange's outer diameter and thickness, the nut's size and thickness, whether the magnetic face sits flush, sticks out, or is recessed relative to the flange, and how long the body is behind the

====== answer ====

1. The male magnetic head sticks out 5mm past the clamp block.
2. The female bulkhead has a diameter of 15mm. The threaded area from the front face to the back stop is 14mm. The threaded area has a flat spot so you can key the hole to prevent rotation. The diameter measurement of this flat spot with calpiers is 14mm. The backstop has a diameter of 20mm and the nut is a hex nut with flat to flat diameter of 19.92mm. The connector is flush with the face. From the back of the flang you'll need at least 40mm of clearance for the built-in strain release and body.
